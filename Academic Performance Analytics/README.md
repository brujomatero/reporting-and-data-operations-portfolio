## Note on Published Data

The data used in the published version of this project was anonymized for portfolio purposes. Student and teacher names were modified to protect the privacy of the individuals involved. Additionally, grades were generated with Python using distributions designed to create realistic academic results without reflecting actual student performance. The data structure, analytical model, and visualizations correspond to the original solution.

## Context

The information related to final exams and grades was distributed across academic schedules, grading records, and management files. Although the data existed, it was not consolidated into a single tool that would allow integrated analysis or provide visibility to different management levels. The absence of centralized indicators made it difficult to monitor academic performance, limiting the availability of information for management meetings and institutional analysis.

## Problem

Academic information related to exams and grades was primarily available at the operational level, making it difficult to access indicators that could provide a comprehensive view of student performance.
The lack of an analytical tool prevented the unified visualization of metrics such as pass, fail, and promotion rates, as well as performance by subject, classroom, and exam session.
As a result, obtaining relevant information often depended on manual searches across multiple data sources, identifying where information was stored, and exchanging files between different areas for updates. This made academic reporting and access to decision-making information slower and less efficient.

## Objectives

- Consolidate information from academic schedules and grading records into a single analytical tool.
- Facilitate access to academic performance indicators for management levels and administrative departments.
- Analyze pass, fail, and promotion rates from different perspectives.
- Compare results across subjects, classrooms, and exam sessions.
- Provide quick-access information for management meetings and academic monitoring.

## Solution

An interactive dashboard was developed to integrate academic information from exam schedules and grading records.
The solution enabled academic performance analysis at different levels of detail, starting with general indicators such as pass, fail, and promotion rates, and extending to more specific analyses by subject, classroom, exam session, and student.
In addition, operational views were incorporated, allowing access to both the complete exam schedule and the detailed list of candidates associated with each subject, centralizing information that had previously been distributed across multiple sources.

## Results

- Academic information previously distributed across multiple files and records was consolidated.
- A reporting tool was created to provide an institutional view of academic performance.
- Indicators for pass rates, fail rates, promotion rates, and academic averages were incorporated.
- Comparative analysis across subjects, classrooms, and exam sessions was facilitated.
- Academic information that had previously been scattered and difficult to access was made available to management levels.

## Tools

- Microsoft Excel for data management and initial data preparation.
- Power Query for data cleansing, transformation, and consolidation of academic information.
- Data Modeling for the integration of exam schedules and grading records.
- Power BI for dashboard development and performance indicator visualization.
- DAX Measures and Calculations for the creation of academic performance metrics.
